1.4.x Stable 包含什么？What is in the 1.4.x Stable line?

1.4.0 将 1.3.1 Beta 线中已验证的 XInput 手柄触发、手柄汇总、任意命名映射层、可选虚拟手柄创建，以及实验性接管的事务/运行健康监测正式带入 Stable。1.4.1 在此基础上只修复界面菜单生命周期问题。

1.4.0 promoted validated XInput triggers, controller aggregation, arbitrary named layers, optional virtual-controller creation, plus the transaction and runtime-health foundation for experimental takeover. 1.4.1 only adds the UI lifecycle fix.
