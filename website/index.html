触发、分层与手柄映射Triggers, layers & controller mapping

查看使用指南Read the guide ↗

触发方式Choose when it runs

按下、松开、按住、短按、长按或双击触发；按键可直接录入，也可从虚拟键盘选择。Run on press, release, hold, short press, long press or double press. Capture a shortcut or choose it on the virtual keyboard.

映射层与独立运行Layers and concurrent macros

把不同用途的宏放进映射层，按需要切换。多个宏可同时运行，也可分别停止。Group macros into named layers and switch between them. Run several macros together and stop them individually.

按应用设置生效范围Limit macros to an application

根据前台进程、窗口标题等条件设置宏的生效范围，在“时间与条件”中统一编辑。Use foreground process or window-title conditions to choose where a macro applies. Edit them under Timing & conditions.

手柄输入与虚拟输出Controller input and output

使用手柄按键、扳机和摇杆触发操作，或将动作输出到虚拟 Xbox 360 / DualShock 4 手柄。Trigger actions from controller buttons, triggers and sticks, or send output to a virtual Xbox 360 / DualShock 4 controller.
